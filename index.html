The tax year, one date at a time

Vincent Lim FCCA runs a small ACCA-registered practice in south-west London. He keeps the HMRC and Companies House dates for sole traders, landlords and limited companies, and explains them in English, 普通話, 廣東話 or 閩南話.

    Last day to use this year's ISA allowance, pension relief and capital gains exemption.

Vincent founded VNW in 2005. He started out as an analyst programmer, moved into accountancy after his first degree in 1992, and has since worked in practice and in catering, IT and charities. He has been company secretary of a listed company and is a director or company secretary of several businesses today.
